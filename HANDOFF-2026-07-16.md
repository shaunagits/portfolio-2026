## Why, where it isn't obvious

**The S mark isn't just the favicon dropped in.** Two things were needed:
- The favicon is a 1500×1500 canvas but the mark's ink is only `1237×1185+136+166` —
  ~20% padding. `LogoS.astro` trims the viewBox to the ink, so a CSS height is true mark
  height. (Same trap as both client logos: art files lie about their size.)
- `fill="currentColor"` replaces the hardcoded `#2C8C99`. **This is load-bearing** — the
  nav swaps to a teal bar at the footer, and a hardcoded teal mark would vanish into it.
  It now inherits `.nav-logo`'s colour and flips to white automatically.

**The hero line moved because the nav lost the name.** "Hi, I'm Shauna" replaced the role
trio, which was already the opening of the subhead directly beneath it ("Designer and
developer.") — the slot was saying it twice. The footer keeps the "Shauna." wordmark;
that's where the full name lives now.

**The secondary button was a ring, not a solid.** It stacked two shadows — a teal shape
plus a `--paper` knockout at `-1.5px` spread that hollowed it out. The knockout is gone.
It uses `--ink` (matching its own border) and reserves the accent for `.btn-primary`, so
the two buttons stop competing side by side.

**The form field was our taxonomy, not the client's.** "Web Development / Web App / AI
Tool / Other" asks a client to sort themselves into service buckets they don't know.
The new options say the outcome. Two deliberate additions:
- *"Fixing or improving my current site"* — probably the most common real enquiry, and
  it had no option before (those people picked "Other", which tells you nothing).
- *"Not sure yet — let's talk"* — a real answer for someone early, and often the best
  fit. Without it they guess wrong or bounce.
The field `name` is still `project-type`, so existing Formspree submissions stay
comparable.

## Traps found (worth not re-learning)

**Astro scoped styles don't reach child components.** `.nav-mark { height: 38px }` in
`Nav.astro` compiled to a scoped selector that couldn't match the `<svg>` — because the
svg is rendered by `LogoS.astro` and never gets Nav's scope attribute. It silently
computed to `height: 0` and **the logo disappeared entirely**. It needs
`:global(.nav-mark)`. This will bite again on the next component that takes a `class`.

**`CLAUDE.md` had `--ink #000000`. It's actually `#0B1416`** — a deliberate hue-shifted
near-black. Corrected. Anyone reading the old line would reach for pure black and quietly
break the palette.

**Transitions lie to `getComputedStyle`.** Reading a style immediately after a state
change returns the *resting* value mid-transition. Two false negatives came from this.
Wait ~600ms, or test `:focus-visible` (same rule block as `:hover`, and focus persists
where hover doesn't).

## OPEN — needs a decision

**The teal footer bar fails WCAG AA, and not just on one element.**

`.nav.is-over-dark` sets the bar to `--primary` (#2C8C99) with white content. White on
that teal is **3.95:1** — below the 4.5:1 that 14px text needs. That's every link on the
bar (Work, About, Services) plus the CTA's hover state, not one component.

`CLAUDE.md` claims *"the accent is dark enough for it"* — true for large text, not for
14px labels.

**Cleanest fix: make that bar `--accent-ink` (#1F6A75) instead of `--primary`.** White on
it is **6.22:1**, it's a colour already in the system, everything on the bar passes at
once, and the bar just gets slightly deeper. One line.

Not done: it's a visible change to a band that was designed deliberately, and it was
outside the ask.

*(Already fixed, same family: the nav CTA's rest label was `--primary` on white at 14px —
3.95:1, and it violated CLAUDE.md's own "don't use --primary for small labels" rule. It's
`--accent-ink` now, 6.22:1.)*
